Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This policy explains how Fusion4 Events uses cookies and similar local-storage technologies, and how you can control them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data saved in your browser. They let a website remember your actions and preferences over time.

Categories We Use

  • Strictly necessary — a single local-storage entry that records your cookie choice so we don't ask again. Always active; cannot be disabled.
  • Preference / third-party content — loaded only after you click "Accept all". These enable web fonts and imagery served by Google, Cloudflare and Unsplash, which may set their own cookies or log your IP.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your Choice

"Accept all" enables third-party content. "Reject non-essential" keeps only the strictly necessary entry. You can change your decision at any time below.
