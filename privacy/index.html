6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15).

  • Request erasure — the "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17).
  • Restrict or object to processing (Art. 18 & 21).
  • Receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior processing (Art. 7(3)).
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

To exercise any right, email info@fusion4events.com.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This policy explains how Fusion4 Events uses cookies and similar local-storage technologies, and how you can control them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data saved in your browser. They let a website remember your actions and preferences over time.

Categories We Use

  • Strictly necessary — a single local-storage entry that records your cookie choice so we don't ask again. Always active; cannot be disabled.
  • Preference / third-party content — loaded only after you click "Accept all". These enable stock imagery served by Unsplash, which may log your IP. Web fonts and icons are self-hosted on fusion4events.com — no font requests go to Google or Cloudflare.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your Choice

"Accept all" enables third-party content. "Reject non-essential" keeps only the strictly necessary entry. You can change your decision at any time below.

Browser Controls

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect how some parts of the site display.
